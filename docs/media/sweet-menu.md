# Sweet menu source assets

The `/sweets` photo menu uses the 36 user-provided images in the repository-root `sweet` folder. Originals remain unchanged. Public images are WebP copies with a maximum width of 1000 px; no image was generated.

Names and prices live in the canonical `src/data/menu/desserts.ts` catalog. The photo-menu view selects entries with `/images/sweet-menu/` images and uses the existing dessert grouping.

Five existing prices were reconciled to the supplied filenames: Crema Catalana, Sorbetto al Limone, Rabbit, Pirata and Principessa. Four catalog entries were added: Chocolate & Hazelnut Cup, Rubino Cheesecake, Traditional Savoiardi Tiramisu, and Cream & Pistachio Cup. Ingredients and allergens were not inferred for these new entries; the page directs guests to the serving team.
